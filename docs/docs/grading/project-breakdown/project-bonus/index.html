Optional Team-Project Bonus #

The required course components total 100%. A team can earn up to 5 additional course percentage points for exceptional work beyond the complete required team project, making 105% the maximum possible course score. The individual project has no separate bonus. Both CS 4641 · Undergraduate and CS 7641 · Graduate teams have the same five-point bonus ceiling, but bonus work must exceed the requirements for their course level. Full required-project credit does not depend on earning a bonus.

How to request it #

On the final team website, add a short Optional bonus section. For each claimed contribution, name the category below, explain what goes beyond the required work, link or show direct evidence, and identify the members who did it. The TA may test a working artifact or ask any member about a claim during the final interview. No separate submission or deadline is added. One feature cannot earn points in two categories.

Categories (up to +1 point each) #

  1. Advanced technical contribution: a substantive new method, difficult integration, or well-justified algorithmic improvement. A larger model or simple API wrapper alone does not qualify.
  2. Exceptional experimental depth: useful ablations, robustness or sensitivity tests, uncertainty analysis, or careful comparisons that materially strengthen the conclusion. More model runs alone do not qualify.
  3. Responsible, secure, or interpretable AI: evidence-based fairness, privacy, security, safety, explainability, or environmental analysis beyond the required discussion.
  4. Software product or meaningful deployment beyond the required visualization: a working application, decision-support tool, or deployed service that uses the team’s model in a useful workflow. The required interactive visualization, a static report, a mockup, or decorative animation does not qualify by itself.
  5. Original data, external evaluation, reproducibility, or open contribution: a responsibly created dataset, substantial stakeholder evaluation, reusable component, or unusually strong reproducibility package beyond the required GitHub code.

Each category is capped at +1 course percentage point; partial credit is possible. Claims need verifiable evidence and compliance with privacy, licensing, and Institute rules. Bonus credit is not guaranteed, and extra polish cannot substitute for a sound required project. Project awards are separate recognition.
