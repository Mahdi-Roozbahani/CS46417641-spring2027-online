Honorlock and Proctored Assessments #

This page covers proctoring setup and technical incidents. See Quizzes and Exams for assessment content, permitted materials, and the midterm reference-sheet policy.

Prepare with Quiz 0 #

Before taking any graded quiz or the online midterm exam, open Quiz 0, answer its practice question(s), and complete the submission using the same computer, browser, camera, microphone, and internet connection you will use for that assessment. Confirm that Honorlock launches correctly and that your equipment and internet connection are working reliably. Quiz 0 is ungraded.

Use Quiz 0 to identify and resolve setup problems before starting a graded attempt. Do not start a graded quiz or the midterm exam with an unresolved problem. A successful practice attempt cannot guarantee that no later outage will occur.

You are responsible for your equipment, internet connection, and testing readiness. The teaching team is not responsible for problems with your personal setup. A technical problem does not automatically entitle you to a restart, extra time, a make-up, or credit for a quiz or midterm exam you could not complete. Unless an exception is officially approved, an uncompleted quiz or midterm exam receives no credit. For an incident during an attempt, follow technical support instructions. For an accommodation or exceptional circumstance, use the official approval and Hub-document process; contacting staff is not a substitute for that process.

Before each assessment, confirm that your camera and microphone work, disconnect external monitors and unauthorized devices, close communication, recording, remote-access, and AI applications, and launch from the Honorlock-enabled Canvas link.

Websites during an assessment #

Do not browse websites during a quiz or the midterm. The only sites permitted in the proctored browser are Canvas/Honorlock and, for the midterm only, the authorized ML Class Hub link to your own reference sheet. Other Hub pages are not permitted. Do not use a second device or another browser to reach a blocked site. If the reference-sheet link does not open during the midterm, contact Honorlock live support immediately instead of bypassing the restriction, then report the incident to your assigned mentor in a private Ed post after the attempt.

Access-code or technical problem #

For Honorlock problems, open your course in Canvas, select Honorlock, and use its built-in support/chat control. During an assessment, use the support control in the active Honorlock window and follow the proctor’s instructions. Keep the support case number for any incident report. Do not leave the assessment to visit an external support website or use an unauthorized device.

If Canvas asks for an access code, do not request one from another student or the team. Exit before beginning, confirm Chrome and the Honorlock extension are active, then relaunch through the Honorlock-enabled Canvas link. Contact Honorlock live support if the problem continues.

If a problem occurs during an attempt, contact Honorlock live support immediately, follow its instructions, preserve the case number, and submit a private Ed post to your assigned mentor promptly afterward with the assessment name, time, and factual description. Do not open an unauthorized app or device to troubleshoot. A technical incident does not automatically grant another attempt; the team reviews the available records.

Assessment security and review #

Do not bypass or interfere with Honorlock using a virtual machine, remote desktop, screen sharing, second device, unauthorized browser environment, or another method. Suspected violations are referred to the Georgia Tech Office of Student Integrity under Institute procedures. An automated Honorlock flag alone is not a misconduct finding; the team reviews relevant recordings and logs.

Approved disability accommodations are implemented with the Office of Disability Services. See General Course Policies for the approval and Hub-document process.
