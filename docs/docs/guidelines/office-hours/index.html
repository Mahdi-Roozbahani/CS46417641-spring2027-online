Online Office Hours #

Office hours are optional online help sessions. They do not replace scheduled project reviews.

Joining a session #

  1. A TA announces the session in an Ed thread with the time and any focus topic.
  2. Reply with a short, specific question and what you have tried. Do not post private information, complete code, grades, or confidential project data.
  3. The TA answers in the thread when useful to the class, or sends a private meeting link when screen sharing or individual discussion is needed.
  4. The TA works through the queue as time allows and posts when the session ends. Joining the queue does not guarantee service before the session ends.

Come prepared with the relevant lecture, error, small code example, or project requirement. TAs can explain concepts, isolated errors, and requirements; they do not debug an entire repository, design a project for a team, provide active assessment answers, or pre-grade a submission. See Ed Discussion for posting and privacy rules.
