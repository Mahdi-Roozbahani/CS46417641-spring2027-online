# Class Resources

## Main Course Reference

**The main reference for this course is *Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning* (PRML) by Christopher M. Bishop.** The course is based primarily on this book, with lecture materials and assigned readings providing additional coverage. A free [official PDF](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2006/01/Bishop-Pattern-Recognition-and-Machine-Learning-2006.pdf) is available, so purchasing a printed copy is not required. Complete the readings assigned for each class.

## Books and Additional References

- [Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning (PRML)](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/pattern-recognition-machine-learning/), by Christopher M. Bishop — **main course reference**; [free official PDF](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2006/01/Bishop-Pattern-Recognition-and-Machine-Learning-2006.pdf).
- [Learning from Data](https://amlbook.com/), by Yaser S. Abu-Mostafa, Malik Magdon-Ismail, and Hsuan-Tien Lin — official book website.
- [Machine Learning](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~tom/mlbook.html), by Tom Mitchell — author's book website and resources.
- [Data Mining: Concepts and Techniques](https://hanj.cs.illinois.edu/bk3/), by Jiawei Han, Micheline Kamber, and Jian Pei — third-edition companion website.
- [The Elements of Statistical Learning](https://hastie.su.domains/ElemStatLearn/), by Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani, and Jerome Friedman — authors' website with a free PDF.
- [Deep Learning](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/), by Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, and Aaron Courville — free online book.

The additional books are supplementary references, not required purchases.

## Dataset Ideas

**May need API, or scraping – thanks to [Polo](https://poloclub.github.io/polochau/) and everyone who contributed with suggestions to these datasets:**

- [HuggingFace Datasets](https://huggingface.co/datasets). [Thanks to Xuhui Zhou]
  Popular dataset-hosting website for machine learning, especially for natural language processing problems. The unified API is convenient for training models.
- [Google Dataset Search](https://datasetsearch.research.google.com)
- [Data Commons](https://datacommons.org/)
- [Kaggle public datasets](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets)
- [Awesome Public Datasets](https://github.com/awesomedata/awesome-public-datasets)
- [NYC TLC trip records](https://www.nyc.gov/site/tlc/about/tlc-trip-record-data.page): taxi and for-hire vehicle trip data.
- [Georgia Tech’s campus data (has APIs):](https://rnoc.gatech.edu/gt-places-api) campus places and building information.
- [Data.gov](https://data.gov/): U.S. Government’s open data
- [IPEDS data](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/use-the-data): Postsecondary education data from National Centre for Education Statistics
- [Bureau of Labor Statistics data](https://www.bls.gov/data/)
- [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Data_access): structured knowledge with query and download options.
- [Yelp Open Dataset](https://business.yelp.com/data/resources/open-dataset/): reviews and business data for educational use; check the dataset terms.
- [OpenAlex](https://help.openalex.org/data/): scholarly works, authors, institutions, and citation data.
- [Numerous APIs from Google](https://developers.google.com/apis-explorer/#p/) (e.g., YouTube)
- [Zillow Research housing data](https://www.zillow.com/research/data/).
- Graph datasets: [SNAP](https://snap.stanford.edu/data/index.html).
- Movies data: [IMDb non-commercial datasets](https://data.imdb.com/non-commercial-datasets/).
- [List of lists of datasets for recommendations](https://gist.github.com/entaroadun/1653794).
- [Million Song Dataset](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.15146/R3467H): audio features and metadata; see the repository for available files.
- [Dataset about soccer games, players, clubs.](https://www.soccerbase.com/)
  Check the site’s access and reuse terms before collecting data.
  For a soccer player: transfer history, performance, nationality, birth date, etc.
  For a soccer club: performance, squad, etc.
- [Nasdaq Data Link](https://data.nasdaq.com/): financial and economic data; availability and access terms vary.
- [UCI Machine Learning Repository](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/datasets): datasets for classification, regression, and other tasks.
- [Amazon AWS Public Data Sets](https://registry.opendata.aws/)
- [KDD Cup](https://www.kdd.org/kdd-cup): data-mining competitions.
- Bibliographic data: [DBLP XML downloads](https://dblp.org/xml/).
- [Retrosheet: MLB statistics (Game/Play logs)](https://www.retrosheet.org/)
- [Classification datasets](https://sci2s.ugr.es/keel/category.php?cat=clas&order=clas#sub2)
- [NOAA NCEI data products](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products): environmental and geophysical data.
- [Pew Research Center datasets](https://www.pewresearch.org/datasets/).
- [Academic torrents (terabytes)](https://academictorrents.com/)
- [New York Times Article Search API](https://developer.nytimes.com/docs/articlesearch-product/1/overview).
- [Case Western Reserve Geotechnical Database](https://engineering.case.edu/geodatabase).
- [Data Science Initiative – Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/academic-program/data-science-microsoft-research/) has various datasets and access to tools that can aid in data science research

Other resources, such as machine learning toolboxes and datasets, will be provided throughout the course.
