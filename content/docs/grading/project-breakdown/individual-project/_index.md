# Individual Project

## From mathematical concepts to real data

**The purpose of the individual project is to bring the mathematical concepts you learn in this course to life by applying them to one real-world data problem.** You will connect the ideas behind an algorithm to the decisions needed to make it work with actual data: what the data represents, which assumptions are reasonable, and how those assumptions affect the results.

Working with the assigned dataset gives you a place to investigate the problem in depth. Examine the data's quality, scale, noise, and structure. Explore how preparing the data, choosing features, and changing its representation affect what an algorithm can learn. Use the assigned clustering and prediction tasks to connect these observations to the concepts studied in class.

Treat this as a process of investigation and experimentation. Try different algorithms and settings, adapt your approach to the problems you find, and compare your experiments fairly. When a method produces unexpected or weak results, investigate why: does the issue come from the data, the representation, the method's assumptions, or the way you evaluated it? Use the mathematical ideas from class to explain the behavior you observe and guide your next experiment.

**The goal is to develop your own understanding and judgment:** to explain your choices, identify limitations, and learn how to improve an approach using evidence. Working through one problem yourself builds a repeatable way of thinking that you can apply to other datasets and real-world problems.
