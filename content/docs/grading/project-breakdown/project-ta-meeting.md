# Required TA Progress Meeting - 5%

This is the first of exactly two required TA interactions. It takes place after the project proposal and before the midterm project report.

**Every team member must attend and must be prepared to answer individual questions about any part of the project. The individual Q&A is graded separately for each student. A student who cannot demonstrate sufficient understanding of the project may receive a lower score than the other members of the same team.**

Saying that a question relates to another team member's part is not an acceptable substitute for understanding the project.

## Purpose

The meeting is designed to:

* Confirm that the team has made meaningful progress beyond the proposal stage.
* Identify technical, organizational, or team-related problems early.
* Review the team's current data, baseline, implementation, and preliminary results.
* Give the team an opportunity to ask focused project questions.
* Verify that every member understands the complete project.
* Check the authenticity, relevance, and correct use of cited sources.
* Establish a clear and realistic plan for the midterm report.

## Meeting Format

The normal meeting length is approximately 15 minutes. The assigned TA will manage the time and may adjust the order when necessary.

### 1. Team Progress Update and Questions

The team should briefly summarize:

* Current project status.
* Data collection, access, cleaning, or preprocessing.
* Implemented baseline, model, or experimental pipeline.
* Preliminary results or evidence of technical progress.
* Important changes from the original proposal.
* Current blockers or team-health concerns.
* Specific questions for the TA.
* Planned work before the midterm report.

The update should be focused and should not repeat the entire proposal.

### 2. Individual Project-Wide Q&A

The TA will direct questions to individual students. The student selected by the TA must answer the question without relying on another team member to answer first.

Questions may cover any part of the project, including:

* Motivation and problem definition.
* Data sources, preprocessing, and limitations.
* Baselines, models, and technical choices.
* Training and evaluation design.
* Metrics and statistical interpretation.
* Preliminary results and failure cases.
* Code structure and reproducibility.
* Team members' contributions.
* Responsible-AI considerations.
* Generative-AI use.
* Any paper, dataset, website, software package, or other source cited by the team.

**The TA may select any cited source and ask any team member to explain:**

* The main idea or contribution of the source.
* How the source relates to the project.
* Which project claim or decision it supports.
* Whether the team reproduced, modified, or rejected the source's approach.

Every student is responsible for understanding the complete project, even when the team has divided the implementation work among its members.

## Required Preparation

Before the meeting, the team must provide the assigned TA with the following materials using the submission method announced by the instructional team:

* Current project website or project summary page.
* A working repository link accessible to the teaching team.
* A concise progress summary of no more than one page.
* A baseline result, preliminary output, or clear evidence of implementation progress.
* An updated task and contribution table.
* An updated Citation Verification Table.
* A short list of focused questions for the TA, when applicable.
* Private team-health information, if requested by the instructional team.

The repository, website, and supporting documents must be accessible before the meeting begins. Time lost because the team did not provide working access will reduce the amount of time available for discussion and Q&A.

## Grading - 5 Course Points

| Criterion                                                                                               | Scope      |  Points |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------: |
| Evidence of meaningful progress, preparation, organization, and a realistic plan for the midterm report | Team       |     2.0 |
| Ability to answer individual project-wide questions accurately and demonstrate genuine understanding    | Individual |     3.0 |
| **Total**                                                                                               |            | **5.0** |

The 2-point team score is normally shared by all team members.

The 3-point individual Q&A score is assigned separately to each student and may differ among members of the same team.

A student's individual score may be reduced when the student:

* Cannot explain important parts of the project.
* Cannot explain the team's data, baseline, model, evaluation, or preliminary results.
* Cannot explain a cited source selected by the TA.
* Relies on another member to answer.
* Repeats memorized language without demonstrating understanding.
* Cannot identify how a reported result was produced.
* Gives answers that conflict with the submitted code, report, or experimental evidence.
* Claims that the question concerns only another member's work.

One incomplete answer does not automatically result in zero credit. The score will reflect the student's overall demonstrated understanding during the meeting.

## Forty-Eight-Hour Follow-Up Video

When a student's answer is incomplete or incorrect, the TA may, at the TA's discretion, allow the student to submit one follow-up video.

The follow-up opportunity is not automatic and is not a substitute for preparing for the live meeting.

The TA will identify the specific questions or topics that the student must address. The video must:

* Be submitted within **48 hours after the scheduled meeting**.
* Show the student on camera.
* Answer the questions assigned by the TA.
* Explain the answers in the student's own words.
* Demonstrate understanding rather than simply reading prepared text.
* Be submitted privately through the method specified by the TA.

A follow-up video may recover up to **50% of the individual Q&A points lost during the meeting**. It cannot provide more than full credit for the individual Q&A component.

Failure to submit the video within 48 hours means that the original individual Q&A score remains unchanged.

## Attendance

All team members are expected to attend the scheduled meeting.

A student with an officially approved exceptional circumstance will be given an alternative individual Q&A process determined by the teaching team. The student must follow the course's Exceptional Circumstances policy.

A student who misses the meeting without an approved exception may receive zero for the 3-point individual Q&A component. An unapproved absence does not automatically qualify for a follow-up video or an individual rescheduled meeting.

## Meeting Outcome

At the end of the meeting, the TA may identify required corrections, action items, or questions that the team should address in the midterm project report.

The meeting is intended to provide direction and verify progress. It is not an approval of the final technical approach, a guarantee of a particular project grade, or a full code-debugging session.
