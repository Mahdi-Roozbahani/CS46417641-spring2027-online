# Individual and Team Projects

These are **two separate projects**, each with its own website link in [ML Class Hub](https://ml-class.cc.gatech.edu/). Keep each link throughout the semester and update the appropriate site at its checkpoints. Dates are on the [course schedule](../../course-info/course-schedule-mahdi/); grade weights are in [Grading Categories](../categories/).

> ### Individual project
>
> Work on your **own** using the dataset assigned in ML Class Hub. Follow the required analysis and website layout. Start with the [Individual Project guide](individual-project/).

> ### Team project
>
> Work with your **registered team** on a question and lawful dataset your team chooses. Use one team website from proposal through final. Start with the [Team Project guide](team-project/).

## Midterm and final: what to submit

There are **two live TA interviews**: the team midterm interview and the individual final interview.

Before attending either interview, read [Guidelines → Project Interview Rules](../../guidelines/project-interviews/).

| Checkpoint | Individual project | Team project |
|---|---|---|
| Midterm | One unlisted YouTube video per student; no interview | Live team interview; no video |
| Final | Live individual interview; no video | One unlisted YouTube video for the entire team; no interview |

Keep updating your existing project websites. The video and interview requirements do not replace the website deadlines.

### Where to submit video links

Open [your projects in ML Class Hub](https://ml-class.cc.gatech.edu/projects/) and select the correct course and checkpoint:

- **Individual midterm video:** each student saves their own unlisted YouTube link in their individual midterm project.
- **Team final video:** one team member saves one shared unlisted YouTube link in the team's final video project. Other members do not submit separate copies.

Use **Save or replace video link** on the relevant project page. Confirm that the saved link appears, test playback while signed out of YouTube, and keep the video available through grading. Choose **Unlisted**, not Private; anyone with the link can watch and reshare it. These are separate submissions: do not put a team video in the individual checkpoint.

The two websites, submissions, and review requirements are different. Use the guides above to find the instructions for the project you are working on.
