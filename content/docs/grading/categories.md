# Grading Categories

## Required Course Grade

The required course components total 100%.

| Component | Weight |
|---|---:|
| 14 graded weekly quizzes | 20% |
| Midterm exam | 12.5% |
| Final exam | 12.5% |
| Required semester project | 55% |
| **Required total** | **100%** |

## Optional Project Bonus

Students may earn up to **5 additional course percentage points** for exceptional optional work in the semester project.

The bonus is added on top of the required 100%. It is not included inside the required 55% project.

- Required coursework: 100%.
- Optional project bonus: up to +5%.
- Maximum possible final course score: **105%**.

## Weekly Quizzes: 20%

The course has Quiz 0 and 14 graded quizzes.

- Quiz 0 is an ungraded Honorlock and course-readiness assessment.
- Quizzes 1 through 14 collectively contribute 20% to the final course grade.
- Exact quiz availability, duration, and point values appear in Canvas.
- Quizzes are closed book, closed notes, individually completed, and Honorlock-proctored.

Quiz questions are based on the assigned lecture videos, lecture slides, and written lecture notes. The ungraded problem sets and practice quizzes are supplementary study resources.

## Midterm Exam: 12.5%

The midterm covers the first part of the course, through clustering evaluation. The primary sources are the assigned lecture videos, lecture slides, and written notes for that portion of the course. Problem Sets 1 and 2 are supplementary practice and may help students understand the expected concepts and reasoning.

The midterm is Honorlock-proctored. Students may use only the approved online reference sheet described on the Quizzes and Exams page.

## Final Exam: 12.5%

The final exam tests **100% post-midterm lecture content**.

All final-exam questions are based on post-midterm lecture content. However, post-midterm topics depend on foundational ideas. Students must be able to use the necessary linear algebra, probability, statistics, optimization, and core equations when solving those questions.

The primary sources are the post-midterm lecture videos, lecture slides, and written notes. Problem Sets 3 and 4 are supplementary practice.

The final is Honorlock-proctored. Students may use only the approved online reference sheet.

## Ungraded Problem Sets: 0%

The four former homework assignments are now ungraded problem sets.

- They do not contribute to the course grade.
- They may be submitted to Gradescope for diagnostic results and convenience.
- Any number, score, or percentage displayed in Gradescope is not an official grade.
- Problem-set questions should be asked in the designated Ed Discussion threads.

## Required Semester Project: 55%

| Project component | Course weight |
|---|---:|
| Proposal and team charter | 5% |
| Required TA Progress Meeting, including individual project-wide Q&A | 5% |
| Midterm project report | 10% |
| Final website, report, code, and reproducibility package | 25% |
| Final presentation, including individual project-wide Q&A | 10% |
| **Required project total** | **55%** |

The project is completed in teams of at least five. Generative AI is permitted for the project with disclosure and verification. Every student is responsible for understanding the entire project.

## Project Bonus: Up to +5%

The optional bonus rewards work that is clearly beyond the complete required project. Bonus points are added directly to the final course score and can raise the overall score to 105%.

See the Project Bonus page for criteria and evidence requirements.

## Academic Integrity

The Georgia Tech Academic Honor Code applies to every component. The permitted collaboration and generative-AI rules differ across quizzes, exams, problem sets, and the project, and are stated on the corresponding pages.
