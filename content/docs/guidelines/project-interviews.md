# Project Interview Rules


These rules apply to **both the team midterm interview and the individual final interview**.

- **Camera on:** every participating student must keep their camera on with their face clearly visible throughout the interview, including all members attending a team interview. If an approved accommodation or a technical problem affects this requirement, contact your assigned mentor through a private Ed Discussion post before the interview when possible (see [contact instructions](../ed-discussion/#contact-your-mentor-through-ed-discussion)). Report an unexpected problem to the interviewing TA immediately and follow up privately on Ed.
- **No live AI assistance:** do not interact with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, or any other generative-AI assistant during the interview. This includes browser tabs, phone apps, voice assistants, coding assistants, and tools that listen to questions or generate suggested answers. Close or disable them before joining. Permission to use and disclose AI while developing a project does not permit AI assistance during its interview.
- **Answer from your own understanding:** do not obtain answers through search engines, messaging platforms, another person, or a second device. You may show your project website, code, and results as directed by the TA. In a team interview, each member must answer questions directed to them; teammates must not privately send answers or speak for that member. Team discussion is allowed when the TA invites it.
- **Be ready to demonstrate your work:** the TA may ask you to share the relevant project window, explain code or results, and make a small change live. Close unrelated private content before sharing. Do not share passwords or private documents.
- **Interview observation:** the TA observes participation, responses, and activity visible on camera or the shared screen and may ask you to explain an apparent off-screen interaction. This is human observation, not automated eye tracking. Looking away or moving your eyes alone is not proof of unauthorized assistance. Any concern will be reviewed in context, with an opportunity for the student to explain, under the course's academic-integrity process.
