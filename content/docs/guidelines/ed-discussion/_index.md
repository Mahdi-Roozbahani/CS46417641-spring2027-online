# Ed Discussion

Ed is the official channel for course announcements, class-wide clarifications, questions, and office-hour notices. Join through Canvas, enable notifications, and read pinned posts and megathreads before posting. Use a descriptive title and say which lecture, assessment, or project requirement your question concerns.

## Contact your mentor through Ed Discussion

**Use Ed Discussion for all course questions and communication with teaching staff. Contact your assigned mentor first.** Identify your mentor in your Ed post so it can be routed appropriately. Your mentor will involve another TA or the instructor when needed. If you do not know your assigned mentor, ask the teaching staff in a private Ed post.

Use a private Ed post for personal or sensitive matters. Do not use email, Canvas messages, or ML Class Hub messages to request course support. The Hub remains the place for required submissions and official accommodation documents; use Ed for the accompanying communication. For a problem during a proctored assessment, follow the [Honorlock incident procedure](../honorlock/) first and contact your mentor on Ed afterward.

## Public and private posts

Use a public post when the answer can help classmates, such as a conceptual question, a broken link, or a project-requirement clarification. Use a private post for grades, accommodations, emergencies, team conflicts, technical incidents, or possible misconduct. Do not include medical documents, personal data, complete solutions, or private grades in a public post.

Ask a focused question and explain what you tried. The team can clarify concepts and isolated problems; it does not complete an assignment, choose a project for you, or pre-grade a submission.

Do not share quiz or midterm questions, answers, or screenshots with classmates or in public posts, including after an assessment closes. Direct any necessary assessment-content question to your mentor in a private Ed post; follow the [Quizzes and Exams rules](../../grading/qquizzes-and-exams/).

For how to join an optional help session, see [Office Hours](../office-hours/). For proctored-assessment incidents, follow [Honorlock](../honorlock/) first, then send the private Ed report.
